Education Policy

Designing a policy response to a K-12 or higher-education problem

This challenge asks teams to design a policy response to a K-12 or higher-education problem for a specific state or district context.

Challenge Document Join the Track E Discord Submit Your Project

Key Project Tasks

  1. Select a focus: learning loss/literacy recovery, teacher shortage & retention, school funding formulas, college access/affordability, early childhood education access, or chronic absenteeism.
  2. Ground the problem in state/district-level data (test scores, funding levels, demographic breakdowns).
  3. Review existing policy levers (state funding formulas, certification rules, financial aid programs).
  4. Design a policy proposal with an implementation and evaluation plan.
  5. Identify equity impacts across district wealth, race, and geography (urban/rural).

Possible Approaches

  1. Funding formula — a revised state school-funding formula addressing an identified gap.
  2. Workforce — a teacher recruitment/retention policy (pay, loan forgiveness, certification pathways).
  3. Access — a higher-ed affordability or dual-enrollment expansion policy.
  4. Early intervention — center on early childhood or literacy-by-third-grade policy.

Required Deliverables

  • 4–6 page policy brief with data, recommendation, and implementation plan
  • One-page fact sheet for a school board or state legislature audience
  • Slide deck (8–10 slides)
  • A simple evaluation plan (what metrics would show the policy is working)
  • Process appendix (0.5 page)

Optional Advanced Tasks

  • Interview a teacher, administrator, or student (with consent)
  • Draft model state legislative or board-of-education rule language
  • Simple data dashboard comparing districts/states
  • Cost-per-outcome analysis

Platforms & Tools

  • Data: NCES, state department of education dashboards, EdBuild/school finance data, Census
  • Legislative tracking: state legislature websites, Regulations.gov
  • Drafting/collaboration: Google Docs, shared repo

Judging Criteria

CriterionWeight
Evidence quality & data use25%
Equity analysis (urban/rural, funding, demographic)20%
Feasibility (political + fiscal)20%
Clarity of implementation & evaluation plan20%
Presentation quality15%

Data & Privacy Requirements

  • Use only public, aggregate education data — no individual student records (FERPA-protected data is off-limits).
  • Guardian consent required for interviewing minors; anonymize identities.
  • Disclose AI tool use.

Team & Deadline

  • Teams of 3–5; a current or former educator/administrator is a plus.
  • Suggested timeline: 3–4 weeks.
  • Final submission deadline: November 14, 2026.
