Health Policy Innovation

Translating a health-systems problem into a proposal that could realistically move through government

This challenge asks teams to translate a real health systems problem — access, cost, equity, or workforce — into a policy proposal that could realistically move through a state legislature, Congress, or a federal agency such as CMS.

Challenge Document Join the Track B Discord Submit Your Project

Key Project Tasks

  1. Choose a focus area: rural health access, behavioral health integration, aging/caregiving policy, maternal health, health data interoperability, or drug pricing.
  2. Ground the problem in data (prevalence, cost, disparities by geography/demographics).
  3. Review existing policy levers (state waivers, CMS rules, legislation, payer models).
  4. Design an intervention with an implementation pathway and funding mechanism.
  5. Identify equity impacts and unintended consequences.

Possible Approaches

  1. State-level pilot — propose a Medicaid waiver or state demonstration program.
  2. Federal rule/legislation — draft a CMS rule comment or bill concept with committee jurisdiction identified.
  3. Payment-model — design a value-based care or reimbursement structure.
  4. Community-based — center on a community health worker / social determinants intervention.

Required Deliverables

  • 4–6 page policy brief with problem statement, evidence base, and recommendation
  • Implementation roadmap (who does what, in what sequence)
  • Budget/fiscal note
  • 5-slide leadership briefing deck
  • Process appendix (0.5 page)

Optional Advanced Tasks

  • Patient/provider journey map showing before/after the policy change
  • Interview 1–2 practitioners or advocates (with consent)
  • Draft sample legislative or waiver language
  • Equity impact assessment using a recognized framework

Platforms & Tools

  • Data: CDC WONDER, KFF, CMS data.cms.gov, county health rankings
  • Legislative tracking: Congress.gov, state legislature trackers, Regulations.gov
  • Drafting/collaboration: Google Docs, shared repo

Judging Criteria

CriterionWeight
Evidence quality & data use25%
Feasibility (political + fiscal)20%
Equity analysis20%
Clarity of implementation plan20%
Presentation quality15%

Data & Privacy Requirements

  • No individually identifiable patient data (PHI) — aggregate/public data only.
  • Informed consent required for interviews; anonymize unless attribution is agreed.
  • Treat any shared personal health information as sensitive by default.
  • Disclose AI tool use in an appendix.

Team & Deadline

  • Teams of 3–5; a clinical/public-health background member is recommended.
  • Suggested timeline: 3–4 weeks.
  • Final submission deadline: November 14, 2026.
